Blunschi Labs

Software products for creative work, live shows, and exploratory travel.

Blunschi Labs is the software division of Blunschi Enterprises, LLC. It develops and operates independent products for writers, musicians, bands, and travelers.

SV In Development

StoryVersion

StoryVersion is a writing and story-development product for drafting, revising, and shaping versions of creative work.

The project is built for writers who want structure without losing the human, messy part of making something new.

Visit storyversion.com
MS Early Access

meSetlist

meSetlist helps bands prepare cleaner live shows with fast setlist editing, shared song notes, and band-first permissions.

It is currently structured for invited bands and early users while the product is refined around real rehearsal and gig workflows.

Visit mesetlist.com
ND Planned App

NotDriven

NotDriven is a travel app concept for people who do not always want the fastest route. The goal is to guide scenic, unfamiliar, or more exploratory drives while still getting travelers to their destination on time.

It is being developed for road trips, open afternoons, and anyone who would rather discover the way there.
