About

Built by a small, hands-on company.

Blunschi Enterprises, LLC exists to create and operate useful software products while supporting original music and creative media work.

The company is intentionally small, independent, and product-focused. Current priorities include publishing software under Blunschi Labs, preparing products for mobile and web distribution, supporting users through clear contact channels, and maintaining policies appropriate for public software releases.

Blunschi Enterprises is the parent company for Blunschi Labs and supports the creative media work of Tripp & The Breakers.

  • Business location Kingston, Georgia, United States
  • Primary work Software products, app development, creative media, and music operations.
